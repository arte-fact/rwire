### Added: design-system gallery pages for the theme foundations

- **Tones:** every tone as a pill (ground and ink), with its dot and an
  outlined label; a `solid` toggle shows the solid fills.
- **Canvas & fonts:** the canvas under a card, the reading, interface and
  mono faces, and the three inks.
- **Density:** the controls the density sizes (buttons, icon buttons, the
  theme toggle, chips, an input, tabs), with a header button that switches
  the theme's density (`Theme::current_density()`).
- The gallery itself runs on the R1 theme: system mode with the three-state
  toggle, Nord, `studio`, font stacks.
